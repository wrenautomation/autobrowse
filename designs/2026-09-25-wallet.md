# Wallet: cards, charges, receipts

William, 2026-09-23: "bill stuff with my credit card (and save my debit
card, but heavily prefer not to use it, especially for any subscription
based / online thats not banking / strict)". Per charge: a text, and an
email to his inbox with the invoice and receipt. Card heavily protected.
2026-09-25: "add the backup of the card in the ssm under not the box".

## Shape

- **Store.** `~/.config/autobrowse/wallet.sealed`, AES-GCM under its own
  keychain item (`autobrowse-wallet`), 0600, written through a rename.
  Opening credentials never opens cards. macOS only: the box has no wallet.
- **Backup.** Every add/remove also writes SSM `/wallet/cards/<label>`
  (SecureString, the Mac's IAM user). Both machine roles (autobrowse box,
  wren pg box) carry an explicit Deny on `/wallet*` (`NeverTheWallet`):
  their managed `AmazonSSMManagedInstanceCore` grants `GetParameter` on `*`
  and they decrypt with the SSM key, so without it a shell there reads a
  card. Descriptions (readable fleet-wide) say brand and kind only.
  `wallet backup` rewrites after a failed backup; `wallet restore` pulls.
- **CLI.** `wallet add [label] --kind credit|debit` asks each field in a
  hidden prompt (paste works, like an SSH passphrase), checked as it is
  entered and asked again when wrong; only brand and last 4 echo back.
  `--clipboard` takes one line `number mm/yy cvc [postal] [name]` and
  empties it. Errors never carry a value. `list` shows brand, kind, last 4,
  expiry. Nothing prints more.
- **Identity.** A card is its number. The label defaults to brand-last4
  (`mastercard-4445`); the same number again moves to the new label; a
  label that names another card is refused. 2026-09-25: a credit card
  saved as `main` was overwritten by a debit card 32 s later.
  `wallet history <label>` / `wallet recover <label> <version>` bring back
  any version SSM kept (that is how it came back).
- **Which card.** `pickCard`: credit by default. Debit only when named,
  only on `WALLET_DEBIT_HOSTS`, never when the page reads as recurring
  (`RECURRING`).
- **Placing.** Explore `place{secret:"card.number"}` (or
  `card@<label>.cvc`; fields number, exp, expMonth, expYear, expYY, cvc,
  name, postal). One yes per card and host over the approval channel
  ("put main: Visa credit ••4242 exp 09/30 on host", DEBIT flagged), then
  each field pastes. Audited (`by: place card.<field>`), journaled redacted.
- **Charges.** A spending click a person said yes to reports after it
  settles: page text (redacted, 6k cap) + full-page PNG as the receipt;
  texted over phone/Linq, each naming the card ("Mastercard credit ending
  4445"; a site's kept card by the `cards-on-file` ledger of what was
  placed where), emailed to `RECEIPTS_TO` (else `NOTIFY_TO`) with
  the PNG attached (Gmail send is multipart now), appended to the chained
  `charges.jsonl` with who heard. A failed channel never fails the click.

- **Profiles.** `autobrowse profile set <id>` / `profile list`: name,
  birthday, gender, billing address, sealed beside the wallet (same
  keychain item), backed up to SSM `/wallet/profiles` (under the Deny).
  A card bills to its `owner` (`wallet own`), else the only profile;
  `place{secret:"card.city"}` (address1, address2, city, region,
  regionCode, postal, country, countryName) fills billing from it.

## Where to attack

1. **The Deny is live** (applied 2026-09-25; `simulate-principal-policy`
   says explicitDeny for both roles). A new machine role must copy it.
2. **The outcome word is a regex on the landing page** ("Charge",
   "Declined", "Check" when it reads as neither or both). A merchant that
   says "declined" in a footer reads as "Check", never "Charge". Fixed
   2026-09-25; a model read of the page would be the step past regex.
3. **Invoice forwarding** watches the account's own inbox (the site's
   credential username or codes inbox) for 15 min, every 30 s, and mails
   the match whole as `invoice.eml`. Skipped when that inbox is the
   receipts inbox (his personal one is never read), when the card was
   declined, and lost if the process exits first (the charge is already
   told). Fixed 2026-09-25.
4. **Desktop buys** (App Store) are gated but not reported.
5. **Compiled workflows** cannot place cards (`cards` is explore-only);
   a compiled checkout needs the same yes-per-host path in `boundRunner`.
6. **Virtual cards** (Privacy.com) would make a leaked number worthless;
   still William's vendor call (`virtual-cards-vendor`).
